## Merge notes (for the integrator)
- **main**: merged into `ui/card` at 50ac9b9 (be/logic, be/drawing); ctest 67/67 after the merge.
- **ui/sidebar** conflicts in `MainWindow.cpp` (`applyUiSetting`, `buildUi`) and `DocumentTab.h` (forward declarations). Resolve as:
  1. `DocumentTab.h`: keep `class CommandCard; using CommandDialog = CommandCard;` plus sidebar's `class FeatureListWidget;`.
  2. `buildUi()`: take sidebar's `SidebarHost` and drop the `SidebarBody` / `SimpleCardSlot` wrapper. Then
     `cardSlotAdapter_ = std::make_unique<SidebarCardSlotAdapter<SidebarHost>>(sidebar_); setCardSlotHost(cardSlotAdapter_.get());`
     and connect `SidebarHost::cardDockAvailabilityChanged` to `if (dialog_) dialog_->reposition();` (instead of my `#BrowserDock` `visibilityChanged` hook).
  3. `applyUiSetting()`: keep both branches (`ui/sidebarSide` must call `applySidebarSide()` and the card loop).
  4. `SimpleCardSlot` and its test expectations (`findChild("CardSlot")`, `cardSlotHost()`) keep working with SidebarHost's own `#CardSlot`. Delete `SimpleCardSlot` afterwards.
  5. `scheduleStateRefresh` forces `setFeatureSelectionActive(false)` when no feature input is active; SidebarHost also detects it from the filter (either source expands), so this is harmless.
- **Tutorial anchors**: `prop:operation` and `prop:direction` now match two widgets with the same objectName: the hidden combo (model) and the visible `SegmentedControl`. The resolver must take the visible one (8.2 says so for `tool:` already). A `do` on a segmented control is a click on the cell (`SegmentedControl::cellRect(i)`).
- **Phase 4** (`ui/viewport`): register the dial, view-tool column, breadcrumb, readout, scale bar and quick toolbar as `OverlayLayout::Item`s in `MainWindow::layoutOverlays()` and the card's `setOverlayRegions()`; the card already keeps clear of `Slot::Orientation` and bottom overlays.
